# Privacy (public page copy)

This app stores the minimum needed to run a private 365-day
push-up challenge.

## What we store

- Email, for sign in (Supabase Auth)
- Display name and timezone you choose
- Push-up sets you log (count, time, local calendar day)
- Daily totals derived from those sets

We do not store photos, GPS, heart rate, or your legal name.

## Who can see what

Challenge members can see your display name, daily totals,
streak, whether you hit 100 today, and when that day first
reached 100. They cannot see your email or per-set notes.

## How long

For the challenge plus 30 days, or until you delete your
account.

## Your controls

In Settings you can export a JSON copy of your data or delete
your account. Deletion removes your profile, membership, sets,
and totals.
